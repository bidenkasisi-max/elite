About Abroad Works

We make international hiring simpler, safer, and more transparent for workers who are looking for better opportunities overseas.

We help candidates understand where they are applying, what the role includes, and what support employers provide before departure. Our mission is to reduce confusion and protect workers from fake offers, hidden fees, and unclear terms.

  • Clear job details, duties, and contract length.
  • Verified employer information and job categories.
  • Transparent work arrangements, support, and benefits.
  • Guidance for workers before they accept a job abroad.
